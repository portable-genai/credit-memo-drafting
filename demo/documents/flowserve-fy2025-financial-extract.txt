FLOWSERVE CORPORATION — EXTRACT OF AUDITED ANNUAL FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
Fiscal year ended 31 December 2025 (FY2025)

Source: Form 10-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on
17 February 2026, accession 0000030625-26-000003 (fls-20251231.htm), and the XBRL
company facts published at data.sec.gov for CIK 0000030625. Every figure below is
as filed. Amounts in millions of U.S. dollars.

INCOME STATEMENT
For FY2025 the group reports revenue of USD 4,729.3m, operating income of USD 399.9m
and depreciation and amortisation of USD 95.5m, giving EBITDA of USD 495.4m on the
statutory definition (operating income plus depreciation and amortisation).
Interest expense of USD 77.7m and a tax expense of USD 155.6m give net income of
USD 346.2m.

The group separately discloses realignment charges of USD 58.3m for FY2025, and
USD 49.5m for FY2024. Adding those charges back gives an adjusted EBITDA of
USD 553.7m, which is the basis on which the group presents its own performance.

BALANCE SHEET AT 31 DECEMBER 2025
Total assets of USD 5,708.2m. Current assets of USD 3,042.9m against current
liabilities of USD 1,501.9m. Inventory of USD 789.9m. Cash and cash equivalents of
USD 760.2m. Long-term debt of USD 1,525.2m with a current portion of USD 49.9m,
giving total debt of USD 1,575.1m and net debt of USD 814.9m. Total equity of
USD 2,191.4m.

CASH FLOW AND DEBT SERVICE
Capital expenditure of USD 70.9m. Scheduled repayments of long-term debt principal
falling due within twelve months are USD 49.6m. The group states in its Form 10-K
that it has "scheduled repayments of $9.4 million in each of the next three quarters
and a $12.5 million repayment on December 31, 2026, under our Term Loan".

SEGMENTS
The group reports two segments. For FY2025 the Flowserve Pump Division reports sales
to external customers of USD 3,230.2m and intersegment sales of USD 5.1m; the Flow
Control Division reports sales to external customers of USD 1,499.0m and intersegment
sales of USD 5.5m. Intersegment sales of USD 10.6m are eliminated on consolidation,
which is why the two divisions' external sales total exactly the consolidated
revenue of USD 4,729.3m.
