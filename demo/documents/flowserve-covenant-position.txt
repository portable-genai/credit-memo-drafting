COVENANT POSITION AND PROPOSED TERMS — FLOWSERVE CORPORATION
Prepared for credit committee. Period: FY2025 Q4 (quarter ended 31 December 2025).

PART A — WHAT THE BORROWER REPORTS (as filed)

Source: Form 10-K for the year ended 31 December 2025, accession
0000030625-26-000003, note on long-term debt.

The borrower's existing Second Amended and Restated Credit Agreement carries
financial covenants tested quarterly. The borrower states that it is "required to
maintain debt ratings, comply with leverage and interest coverage financial
covenants", and that the agreement "extended net leverage ratio covenant definition
through the maturity of the agreement" — that is, the leverage covenant is measured
on a NET basis, after deducting cash.

On that definition the borrower reports current net leverage of 1.64x, and states:
"Financial Covenants — Our compliance with the financial covenants under the Second
Amended and Restated Credit Agreement are tested quarterly. We were in compliance
with all covenants as of December 31, 2025."

Available borrowings under its revolving credit facility were USD 615.8m at
31 December 2025.

PART B — THE COVENANTS THIS BANK PROPOSES FOR THE NEW FACILITY

These are our own proposed terms for the facility under consideration. They are not
the borrower's existing covenants and the borrower has not agreed them. They are
measured on GROSS debt, before deducting cash, which is this bank's standing
definition:

  Maximum net leverage covenant of 3.00x, tested quarterly.
  Minimum debt-service coverage ratio (DSCR) of 1.25x, tested quarterly.
  Minimum current ratio of 2.00x, tested quarterly.

Note for the file: the borrower measures leverage after netting cash of USD 760.2m
and after adding back realignment charges of USD 58.3m. This bank does neither. The
difference between the two definitions, and not any difference in the underlying
figures, is what any disagreement between this memo and the borrower's own
certificate will reflect.
